# Commercial site ideas

Three proposals, written on 2026-11-09, for more commercial and more popular sites in English, French and German, built from the four existing directory sites: Alt Web, anglais-gratuit, bon-et-gratuit and Simply Good Food.

**Recommendation:** build three new brands, each drawing on different sites, and start with languages. Alt Web's mission page promises that nobody can pay for a place or a score, so it should stay non-commercial and feed the others only with content and credibility. (Update 2026-11-18: Alt Web now allows clearly marked affiliate links that never change the scores; see [traffic-potential.md](traffic-potential.md). It still sells no places or scores.)

The main change in all three is from browsable cards to pages that match what people search just before they spend: "best X", "X vs Y", "how much does X cost", "which one is right for me".

## 1. Language learning for all three audiences (from anglais-gratuit)

**Idea:** turn the French-only directory into a site on how to reach a level or pass an exam, covering every direction: FR→EN, DE→EN, EN→FR, EN→DE, FR→DE and DE→FR.

- **Popular pages:** Babbel vs Duolingo, how long it takes to reach B2, and exam prep (IELTS, TOEFL, TCF, Goethe, telc). Add the language levels required for citizenship or residence.
- **Revenue:** affiliate links to apps and online tutors, sponsored cards, then our own paid exam-prep packs.
- **Why it fits:** anglais-gratuit already has 121 resources, level and language fields, and ad cards for Babbel and Duolingo. Most guides translate well, and a free level test gives people a reason to visit that an AI summary can't replace.
- **Main risk:** the "gratuit" name won't work, so it needs a new brand and domain.

## 2. Cut your bills, country by country (from bon-et-gratuit and Alt Web)

**Idea:** a site for paying less on electricity, mobile, internet, banking, insurance and transport, plus the benefits and aid people don't claim. Green and cooperative options appear as a visible filter, not the whole pitch.

- **Popular pages:** cheapest mobile plan this month, free bank accounts, switching electricity supplier, and "am I entitled to X" calculators.
- **Revenue:** the largest commissions per sign-up of the three, plus a newsletter of current deals.
- **Why it fits:** it has the largest audience, and Alt Web's country plates already handle "available where".
- **Main risks:**
  - Offers and aid don't translate, so France, Germany and the UK are three separate content sets. On 2026-11-09 Alt Web had 107 French entries, 53 German and 24 UK.
  - Prices need constant updates.
  - The field is crowded (Check24, Verivox, Finanztip, Selectra, MoneySavingExpert).
  - Insurance and credit referrals can require a broker registration, so get that checked first.

## 3. Eat well on a small budget (from Simply Good Food)

**Idea:** a free weekly meal planner: choose a budget, household size and diet, and get a menu and shopping list. The recipes support the planner.

- **Popular pages:** meals for €30 a week, seasonal menus, cooking for one, and what to do with leftovers.
- **Revenue:** display ads, kitchen equipment affiliates (recipes already have tool tags) and supermarket or meal-kit partners, then a paid planner.
- **Why it fits:** it translates fully, it's a weekly habit, and it can run in the browser from data the site already has (price, diet, ingredients).
- **Main risks:**
  - 84 recipes is too few; it needs around 300.
  - Competing directly on recipe searches against Marmiton, Chefkoch or BBC Good Food won't work, so the planner has to be what draws people.
  - Display ads pay little per visit.
  - Accounts for a paid tier would end the purely static setup.

## How they compare

| | Audience | Revenue per visitor | Effort from today | Translates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Languages** | Medium | Good | Low to medium | Mostly |
| **2. Bills** | Largest | Highest | High, ongoing | No |
| **3. Meals** | Large | Low at first | Medium | Yes |

Bills has the biggest upside, but its upkeep is heavy for a small operation. Languages is the quickest way to test whether commercial pages earn anything.

## Before committing

- **Unchecked assumptions:** this was written without looking at the Umami traffic or researching search volumes, and the commission and competitor points come from general knowledge. The ratings in the table are judgement calls. Check them before investing.
- **AI-written content:** the pages are marked `source: AI`, and commercial pages written that way are exposed to Google's quality filters. Each site needs something first-hand, such as real tests, price tracking or a tool.
- **Affiliate links:** they must be clearly labelled in all three countries, and commissions must stay separate from the scores.
